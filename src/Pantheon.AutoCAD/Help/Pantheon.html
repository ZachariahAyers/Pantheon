Pantheon

Draws metal building framing in AutoCAD from the engineer's numbers. Free, made in the USA by Ayers Electronics.

Every command is on the Pantheon ribbon tab. If the tab is missing, type PANTHEON.

  • WORKFRAME — a gable, single slope or off-center ridge workframe with grid bubbles
  • SLAB — a foundation slab, with an optional brick-ledge notch
  • COLUMN — a straight column base plate with anchor holes
  • ZEE, CEE, EAVECEE — girt, purlin, jamb, post and eave strut sections
  • PANTHEONPALETTE — the Shapes and Pitch palettes
  • PANTHEONABOUT — the version, the privacy policy and support

Lengths take feet and inches, such as 25'6". Bay lists read the way engineers write them, such as 3*25',20'. Each member is a named block on its own layer; one undo takes it back.

Try a workframe in your browser at ayers.repair/opensource/pantheon. Questions: support@ayers.repair.
