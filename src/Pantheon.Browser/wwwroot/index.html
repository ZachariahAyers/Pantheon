Add-in / 03

Commands

Every command is on the Pantheon ribbon tab. Type PANTHEON to bring the tab back.

WORKFRAME
A gable, single slope or off-center ridge workframe with frame lines, purlin and girt lines, and grid bubbles.
SLAB
A foundation slab, with an optional brick-ledge notch.
COLUMN
A straight column base plate with its anchor holes.
ZEE
A zee girt or purlin section, at any roof pitch.
CEE
A cee jamb or post section.
EAVECEE
An eave strut in any of its four orientations.
PANTHEON
Shows the ribbon tab and the palettes.
PANTHEONPALETTE
Shows the Shapes and Pitch palettes.
PANTHEONABOUT
The version, the quick start, the privacy policy and support.

Runs in AutoCAD 2027 on 64-bit Windows. Bay lists read the way engineers write them, such as 3*25',20'. Each member is a named block on its own layer.
